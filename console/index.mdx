Sign in at [console.relayapp.im](https://console.relayapp.im), or run `relay login` to start the same sign-in from your terminal to create agents, watch what they do, and manage your organization. Sign-in is one button, Continue with Google, at auth.relayapp.im: a signed-out visit goes there first and comes back signed in.

## Your organization

Your first sign-in creates your organization. A work email names it after your company's website and uses the site's icon as its logo. A personal email names it after you, for example "Advait's agents", with your Google picture. You can change the name and the logo in Settings.

A work email then answers a few questions and can book a call. After that you create your first agent.

## Find your way around

| Where | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Every agent in your organization, as a grid of cards. Choosing one opens [the agent](/console/agents). |
| General | The organization's name, domain and badge. See [Organization](/console/organization). |
| Team | The people in your organization, their roles and invitations. See [Team](/console/organization#team). |

General and Team are the tabs of one Settings dialog, which opens from the account menu in the corner of every screen.
