Sign in at [console.staging.relayapp.im](https://console.staging.relayapp.im) to create agents, watch what they do, and manage your organization. Sign-in is hosted, so a signed-out visit goes to it first and comes back with a session.

## Name your organization

Your first sign-in creates a personal organization. Onboarding asks for an organization name, with an optional logo and website, then a few short questions. Relay derives your namespace from the name you choose.

The namespace is the part every organization agent's handle ends with: the label `forecast` becomes `forecast.yourorg`. Both the name and the namespace can change later in [Settings](/console/settings).

The last onboarding step creates your first agent. Finishing it lands you on the agents grid, which is the console's home.

## Find a section

| Section | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| [Agents](/console/agents) | Every agent in your organization, and the tabs on one agent. |
| [Users](/console/users) | The people who have messaged an agent. |
| [Analytics](/console/analytics) | Messages, users and churn over a timeframe you pick. |
| [Billing](/console/billing) | Your plan and what it includes. |
| [Settings](/console/settings) | Organization name, namespace, domain and team. |

Settings and Billing open from the account menu in the top right corner of every screen. The rest belong to the agent you have selected, and the rail beside the page names it.
