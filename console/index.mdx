Sign in at [console.staging.relayapp.im](https://console.staging.relayapp.im) to create agents, watch what they do, and manage your organization. Sign-in is hosted: a signed-out visit goes there first and comes back with a session.

## Create your organization

Your first sign-in creates a personal organization, and the first step of sign-up names it. Enter an organization name, then a namespace, which Relay fills in from the name and you can edit. A logo and a website are optional.

An organization agent's handle ends with the namespace: the label `forecast` becomes `forecast.yourorg`. Use 2 to 63 lowercase letters, numbers, underscores or hyphens, starting with a letter or a number. `admin`, `dev`, `parked` and `relay` belong to Relay, and a namespace another organization holds is refused.

The last step of sign-up creates your first agent.

## Find your way around

| Where | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Every agent in your organization, as a grid of cards. Choosing one opens [the agent](/console/agents). |
| General | The organization's name, namespace, domain and badge. See [Organization](/console/organization). |
| Team | The people in your organization, their roles and invitations. See [Team](/console/organization#team). |
| Billing | Your plan, and what Relay charges for. See [Billing](/console/organization#billing). |

General, Team and Billing are the three tabs of one Settings dialog, which opens from the account menu in the corner of every screen.
