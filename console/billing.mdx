Billing is a section of Settings, which opens from the account menu in the corner of every screen. Only the organization owner can read it.

## Read your plan

The Plan row names your plan and its price, the date it renews, and how many Premium Handles are in use. An active plan offers **Change plan** and **Manage**. **Manage** opens the billing portal, which owns payment methods, receipts, tax details, invoices, and plan changes.

Without an active plan the row offers **Subscribe** and **See plans**. A payment that is past due says so above the row, and the portal is where you correct it.

## Compare the plans

**See plans** opens Personal, Business and Enterprise beside each other. The comparison carries one row each for Price, Agents, Handles, Premium Handles, Team seats, and People a month. Relay's billing catalog is the authority for every number there, so the dialog reads them live.

## Count the people

People a month is what Relay charges for once the included allowance is spent. Everyone who exchanges a delivered message with one of your agents counts, and each person counts once in a billing period however many messages they send. The graduated rates are read against the month's total people, the included allowance as well.
