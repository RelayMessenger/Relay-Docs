An agent's numbers sit at the top of its Overview, one measure to a tab, and the chart under them draws whichever measure you choose.

## Choose a measure

| Measure | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| Messages | Everything your agents sent and received. |
| Users | People who messaged at least once. |
| Churn | Users who reached one month of inactivity. |

Messages and Users total the window. Churn is a rate, so its number is the window's average rather than its sum. The Users total counts each person once, in the bucket they were last heard from, rather than once for every day they wrote in.

## Choose a timeframe

The dropdown beside the measures holds Last 24 hours, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Last 3 months, Last year and All time. Your plan decides how far back a window may reach: one that reaches past what the plan includes carries a lock and opens the plans instead.

Under the chart are the agent's most recent events. The whole stream is the Events tab.
