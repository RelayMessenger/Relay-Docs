Your organization's settings open from the account menu in the corner of every screen, as a dialog over the page you are on.

## General

The first block is the organization itself: its logo, name and namespace. **Edit** opens the name and the namespace together.

A namespace is 2 to 63 lowercase letters, numbers, underscores or hyphens, starting with a letter or number. `admin`, `dev`, `parked` and `relay` belong to Relay, and one another organization holds is refused. Saving a new namespace renames every organization agent with it.

Below sit a support link and a domain, verified with the DNS record the row hands you. A verified domain can apply for the Verified badge.

## Team

Team lists everyone in your organization with their role, with invitations, role changes, removals and the transfer of ownership. An invitation stays on the list until it is accepted. Seats are free on every plan.

## Billing

Billing is the owner's to read. The Plan row names your plan and price, when it renews, and how many Premium Handles are in use. An active plan offers **Change plan** and **Manage**, where **Manage** opens the billing portal for payment methods, receipts and invoices. Otherwise it offers **Subscribe** and **See plans**, and a payment past due says so above it.

**See plans** compares Personal, Business and Enterprise by Price, Agents, Handles, Premium Handles, Team seats, and People a month. The dialog reads every number live from Relay's billing catalog.

People a month is what Relay charges for once the included allowance is spent. Everyone who exchanges a delivered message with one of your agents counts, once in a billing period, however many messages they send.
