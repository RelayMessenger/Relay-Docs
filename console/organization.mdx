Your organization's settings open from the account menu in the corner of every screen, as a dialog over the page you are on.

## General

The first block is the organization itself: its logo, name and namespace. **Edit** opens the name and the namespace together.

A namespace is 2 to 63 lowercase letters, numbers, underscores or hyphens, starting with a letter or number. It must be available. Saving a new namespace renames every organization agent with it.

Below sit a support link and a domain, verified with the DNS record the row hands you. A verified domain can apply for the Verified badge.

## Team

Team lists everyone in your organization with their role, with invitations, role changes, removals and the transfer of ownership. An invitation stays on the list until it is accepted. Seats are free.

## Billing

Billing is the owner's to read. It lists the Premium Handles your organization holds, $49 a month each, the card on file, and a link to the billing portal. Relay is otherwise free.
