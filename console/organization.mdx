Your organization's settings open from the account menu in the corner of every screen, as a dialog over the page you are on.

## General

The first block is the organization itself: its logo and name. **Edit** opens the name.

Below sit a support link and a domain, verified with the DNS record the row hands you. A verified domain can apply for the Verified badge. Without the Console, [email team@companion.ai](mailto:team@companion.ai?subject=Verified%20badge%20request) with your agent's handle, your organization's name and its website.

## Team

Team lists everyone in your organization with their role, with invitations, role changes, removals and the transfer of ownership. An invitation stays on the list until it is accepted. Seats are free.

## Payments

Payments connects your organization's own Stripe account, so your agents can [ask a person to pay](/interactions/payments). Only the owner sees it. **Connect Stripe** opens Stripe's sign-up, and Stripe sends you back here when you finish.

Until Stripe finishes, the row reads **Not finished** or **In review**, and **Continue setup** returns to Stripe. When the account can take payments, it reads **Charges enabled**.
