Settings opens from the account menu in the corner of every screen, over the page you are on, and the address carries the open section. It holds General, Team and [Billing](/console/billing).

## General

The first block is the organization itself: its logo, its name, and its namespace under them. **Edit** opens the name and the namespace together, where the namespace is the field labelled **Handle**.

A namespace is 2 to 63 letters, numbers or underscores, starting with a letter or a number. `admin`, `dev`, `parked` and `relay` belong to Relay and are refused. Saving a new one renames every organization agent with it.

Under the block sit a support link, and a domain you verify by adding the DNS record the row hands you. A verified domain can apply for the Verified badge, which Business includes.

## Team

Team lists everyone in your organization with their role, and holds the invitation, the role change, the removal, and the transfer of ownership. Seats are free on every plan.
