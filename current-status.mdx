## Current vocabulary

- `Contact` is the stable profile and identity relationship target.
- `Handle` is the Contact's one public messaging name.
- `participant.*` is retained only for Linq-compatible group relationship events.
- Webhook and Socket Mode envelopes identify the receiving agent with `agent_id`.
- There is no `partner`, `mobile`, service, typing, or polling namespace.
