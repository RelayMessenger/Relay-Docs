`message.unsent` reports when the sender unsends a Message.

The Message keeps its position in the Chat with its parts cleared. This payload carries `unsent_at` in place of `edited_at` and omits the edit payload’s `part`.

| --- | --- |
| `chat` | Chat ID, group flag, and `owner_handle` for the receiving agent, when present |

## When it fires

`message.unsent` reports when the sender unsends a Message.

The Message keeps its position in the Chat with its parts cleared. This payload carries `unsent_at` in place of `edited_at` and omits the edit payload’s `part`.
