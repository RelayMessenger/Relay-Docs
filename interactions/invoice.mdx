An `invoice` part is a card that asks the person to pay. It shows your business name, an amount, and a title; tapping it opens your own Stripe checkout link. Relay never touches the money: there is no Relay Stripe account, no processing step, and no Relay fee. You get paid through your own Stripe account; Relay only carries the card and tells the person, through the status you set, when they have paid.

Two rules protect the person paying:

- **Only a verified agent can send an invoice.** Your agent's organization must hold the Verified badge. Apply from [Organization](/console/organization) in the Console, or [email team@companion.ai](mailto:team@companion.ai?subject=Verified%20badge%20request) with your agent's handle, your organization's name and its website. Until then, a send that includes an invoice fails with `403`.
- **The link must be a Stripe checkout page.** Relay accepts only Stripe's own hosted payment pages, so a card can never open a look-alike site.

## Send an invoice

An `invoice` part carries a `title`, an integer `amount` in minor currency units, a `currency`, whether the goods are `physical` or `digital`, and the `url` of your Stripe checkout. Only a verified agent can send an invoice, and it must be the only part in its Message. Tap the card in the demo to see the checkout open; the demo loads nothing from the example URL.

### Fields

- Only a verified agent can send an invoice. A person's attempt, or an attempt from an agent whose organization is not verified, fails.
- A Message carries at most one `invoice` part, and it must be the Message's only part: no text, media, buttons, or selection beside it.
- `title` is 1 to 32 characters after surrounding whitespace is trimmed.
- `amount` is an integer number of minor currency units from 1 to 99,999,999 (Stripe's own ceiling).
- `currency` is a 3-letter ISO code, case-insensitive in, always returned lowercase.
- `goods` is `physical` or `digital`; see [Physical and digital](#physical-and-digital).
- `url` is your Stripe checkout link, up to 2048 characters; see [Checkout links](#checkout-links). Relay stores it normalized, so a mixed-case scheme or host comes back the way Relay wrote it, not the way you sent it.
- `recurring` is optional; see [Subscriptions](#subscriptions).
- Unknown fields are rejected.

## Checkout links

`url` must be an `https` link on one of Stripe's hosted payment pages. Take it from the Stripe API response, never build it by hand.

| Host | Stripe object | Where to get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `checkout.stripe.com` | Checkout Session | The session's `url` |
| `buy.stripe.com` | Payment Link (pay or subscribe) | The Payment Link's `url` |
| `book.stripe.com`, `donate.stripe.com` | Payment Link (book or donate) | The Payment Link's `url` |
| `invoice.stripe.com` | Invoice | The invoice's `hosted_invoice_url` |

Test-mode links use the same hosts, so `https://buy.stripe.com/test_…` works while you build. Relay refuses every other link, including:

- Stripe receipts (`pay.stripe.com`) and the customer portal (`billing.stripe.com`), since neither is a place to pay.
- A Stripe [custom domain](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/checkout/custom-domains) such as `pay.example.com`, since Relay cannot tell it is Stripe's. Use the `checkout.stripe.com` or `buy.stripe.com` link for the same object instead.
- A link with a port, a username or a password. `https://buy.stripe.com@evil.com` goes to `evil.com`.

## Physical and digital

What the card does when it's tapped depends on `goods` and, for digital goods, the App Store storefront the person's device last reported:

| Goods | Storefront | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `physical` | Any | The card opens your checkout `url` in the app's own in-app browser. |
| `digital` | US App Store | The card opens your checkout `url` in Safari, outside the app. |
| `digital` | Any other App Store | The invoice is rejected when you send it. The card is never created. |

<InvoicePreview agent="Nimbus Cloud" title="Pro plan" amount={1900} currency="usd" goods="digital" payable={false} />

Relay checks the storefront the person's device last reported, not their location, and reads it through StoreKit rather than Location Services. A device that has never reported a storefront is let through when you send. On that device the card reads **Unavailable** and does not open, because the app only links out for digital goods when it knows the storefront is the US. This follows Apple App Store Review Guideline 3.1.3(e) (goods and services outside the app for physical purchases) and 3.1.1(a) (in-app purchase for digital goods and services).

## Subscriptions

Add `recurring` to bill on a schedule: an `interval` of `day`, `week`, `month`, or `year`, and an optional `interval_count` (default `1`) to bill every few of them, such as every 2 weeks or every 3 months. The span of `interval` × `interval_count` cannot exceed 3 years. The card shows the amount with a short cadence, for example "$19.00/mo" or "$19.00/3 mo".

<InvoicePreview agent="Nimbus Cloud" title="Pro plan" amount={1900} currency="usd" goods="digital" recurring={{ interval: "month" }} url="https://buy.stripe.com/test_sub_123" />

Relay does not run the subscription. Your Stripe checkout and your Stripe account own the recurring charge, the retries, and the renewal. The card tracks the sign-up, not every charge: set `succeeded` once when the first payment goes through, and the card reads **Subscribed**. Leave it there while renewals succeed, and set `canceled` when the subscription ends.

## Update the status

Move the card's `status` with `PUT /v1/messages/{message_id}/invoice`. Only the agent that sent the invoice can call this; every other identity gets a `403`. Any status can move to any other, as often as you need, and setting the status it already has is a no-op that still returns `200`.

<Note>
A status is only a label on the card. Setting `refunded` or `canceled` refunds nothing and stops nothing. Refund the charge or cancel the subscription in Stripe first, then set the status so the card says so.
</Note>

| `status` | The card shows | Set it when | Stripe webhook event |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `requested` | "Pay" and a chevron. Tapping opens your checkout. This is the default. | You sent the invoice, or want to reopen it. | None |
| `succeeded` | A green check and **Paid**, or **Subscribed** when `recurring` is set. | The payment went through. For a subscription, only the first payment. | `checkout.session.completed` with `payment_status` `paid`, or `invoice.paid` |
| `canceled` | A red x and **Canceled**. | The order was called off, or the subscription ended. | `customer.subscription.deleted` |
| `expired` | A red x and **Expired**. | The checkout link ran out before the person paid. | `checkout.session.expired` |
| `refunded` | **Refunded** in blue. | You refunded the payment in Stripe. | `charge.refunded` |

Only a `requested` card can be tapped. Every other status closes the card: it stops opening your checkout until you set `requested` again.

<InvoicePreview agent="Sonic Coffee" title="House blend, 250 g" amount={2400} currency="usd" goods="physical" status="succeeded" />

Call this from your Stripe webhook handler, not from guesswork about whether the person paid. Each change updates the card for the person straight away.

<CodeGroup>
```typescript TypeScript SDK
import Relay from "@relaymessenger/sdk";

const relay = new Relay({
  apiKey: process.env.RELAY_AGENT_TOKEN!,
  baseURL: "https://api.staging.relayapp.im",
});

await relay.messages.invoice.update(messageId, { status: "succeeded" });
```

```bash HTTPS
curl -sS -X PUT "https://api.staging.relayapp.im/v1/messages/$MESSAGE_ID/invoice" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $RELAY_AGENT_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"status":"succeeded"}'
```
</CodeGroup>

```http
HTTP/1.1 200 OK
```

A real status change pushes a `message.upserted` for the card to every person in the chat; a no-op pushes nothing. There is no separate webhook for this, since you are the one who called it, so you already know.

## Send one from a runtime

Invoices are a property of the Message, so every runtime sends them through the send path it already has: a backend includes the `invoice` part alone in `parts`; a runtime with a send tool takes an `invoice` argument; and a runtime that answers in text ends its answer with a fenced code block tagged `invoice` holding the fields, which the bridge lifts out into its own solo Message. Any words before the fence go out first, as an ordinary text Message, since an invoice cannot share a Message with anything else. Sending more than one invoice fence, or an invoice fence alongside a `buttons` or `selection` fence, in the same answer is an error.

````text
Here's your total for the order.

```invoice
{
  "title": "House blend, 250 g",
  "amount": 2400,
  "currency": "usd",
  "goods": "physical",
  "url": "https://buy.stripe.com/test_123"
}
```
````

Each [integration](/integrations/index) says which of these applies in its "What it can do" table. A runtime only sends the card. Update its status from the backend that receives your Stripe webhooks, with the SDK or HTTPS, as shown in [Update the status](#update-the-status).

## Only people pay

- Only a verified agent can send an invoice.
- Only a person can react to an invoice; an agent's reaction is rejected.
- Only the agent that sent an invoice can move its status; every other identity, including another agent in the same chat, is rejected.
- Invoices work in direct and group chats the same as any other part.
- Replies to an invoice Message are ordinary replies; an invoice has no reply format of its own.

## When it fails

| Status | Code | Cause |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `400` | [1005](/api-reference/errors#1005) | The invoice is not alone in its Message, `title`/`amount`/`currency`/`goods` fails validation, `url` is not a [Stripe checkout link](#checkout-links), `recurring`'s span exceeds 3 years, or the status route's `status` is not one of the five known values. |
| `403` | [2003](/api-reference/errors#2003) | A person or an unverified agent sent an invoice, an agent reacted to one, or an identity other than the sending agent called the status route. |
| `404` | [2001](/api-reference/errors#2001) | The status route's message does not exist, is not visible to the caller, or carries no invoice part. |
| `422` | [2006](/api-reference/errors#2006) | A digital invoice would go to a person whose device last reported a non-US App Store storefront. |
