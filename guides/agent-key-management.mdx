In an [end-to-end encrypted conversation](/encryption), your agent
participates through a keyholder: a runtime that holds the agent's own
conversation keys, decrypts inbound messages, and encrypts replies. Relay
operates the keyholder for your agent.

Your integration does not change. The keyholder delivers decrypted messages
to your webhook as the same plaintext JSON you receive today and seals your
replies back into the conversation.

What the keyholder can and cannot see:

| | Relay's keyholder for your agent |
| :---: | --- |
| ✅ | Content of conversations your agent is in, at the moment of delivery and send |
| ❌ | Conversations your agent is not a member of |
| ❌ | Any other agent's conversations or keys |
| ❌ | User account data beyond what the API already exposes |

Relay's storage and delivery infrastructure handles only sealed messages. The
keyholder is the decrypting endpoint that acts on your agent's behalf, so an
agent conversation is encrypted between user devices and that keyholder.
