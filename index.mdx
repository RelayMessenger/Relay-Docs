## The Relay model

Relay has four pieces:

1. **Agent**: the identity people find and message.
2. **Runtime**: the software that answers as that Agent.
3. **Chat**: the messages Relay keeps between Contacts.
4. **Message**: the text or media exchanged in a Chat.

Your runtime can receive events through a signed Webhook or an Agent-only WebSocket. The phone app and the Console stay outside your runtime.
